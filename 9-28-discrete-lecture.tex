We assume the opposite of what is being claimed and we show that assumption is false. Assume the premis but not the conclusions 
\\
\\
Lets work with $p \iff q$
\\
\\
The process is: \\
1. assume the negation is true. $\lnot p \iff q$ \\
Recall that $\lnot p \iff q \equiv \lnot (p \lor q)$ \\ 
Also recall that we can apply DeMorgans law to this logical equivilancy to get: \\
$\lnot (p \lor q) \equiv p \land \lnot q$ \\ \\

Applying this to "If $n^2$ is even then $n$ is even" we get \\
