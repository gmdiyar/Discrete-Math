\section{Introduction}

If $x^2+5x$ is odd than $x$ is odd.

\begin{proof}

Contrapositive: if $x$ is even than $x^2+5x$ is even

\begin{lemma}
x is even if and only if $\exists K \in \mathbb{Z}, 2K = x$
\end{lemma}

Since $x = 2k$, we substitute $x$ for $2k$ in $x^2+5x$

$(2k)^2+5(2k)$
$4k^2+10k$

We can factor out a $2$ from this expression

$2(2k^2+5k)$ 

The expression being multiplied by $2$ is an integer.
Therefore, since $x$ is even (2k), $x^2+5x$ is even.

QED the contrapositive is true so the original claim is also true.

\end{proof}
